Privacy policy

The OAuth applications registered under knobla.com are personal tools used only by the domain's owner. They are not offered to the public.

  • They access only the owner's own Google account (for example, to upload the owner's photos to their own Google Photos library).
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold or shared with anyone. Access tokens stay on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/CreRecombinase/knobla.com.
